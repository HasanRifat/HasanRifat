## Premise

A *khizānah* is a storehouse; the old libraries were called *khizānat al-kutub*. The modern version of the problem is that the material is everywhere — PDFs, lecture series, notes, printed books — and none of it knows about the rest.

## What I'm doing

Building the catalogue and the structure underneath it: what an entry is, how a book relates to its explanations and to the lectures given on it, and how to find where you stopped.
