## What it was

A moderation bot for Discord servers: a moderation system, infraction logging, and a drawer of small utilities — including, inevitably, a coin flip.

## How it was built

Node.js with the Discord.js library, written in an object-oriented style, hosted on DigitalOcean and running on several servers at the time.

## Why it is still on the shelf

It is where I learned that software in use is a different thing from software that works.
