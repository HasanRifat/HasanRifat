## Premise

A *dīwān* was a register: the place where an administration wrote things down so they could be found again. Mine is a single surface for the things I would otherwise keep in six apps and my head.

## What I'm doing

Designing and building it for an audience of one. That constraint is the point: no settings page, no onboarding, nothing made for a hypothetical user.

## Direction

A dashboard first, an assistant second. The assistant should earn access to one thing at a time, after the manual version has proved worth keeping.
