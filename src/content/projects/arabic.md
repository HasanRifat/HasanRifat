## Premise

Arabic rewards structure. Three root letters and a small set of patterns produce most of the vocabulary; learn the patterns and the language starts explaining itself. Most learning tools hide that and hand you flashcards.

## What I'm doing

Laying out the sequence — what has to come before what — and building the exercises around the patterns rather than around word lists.
