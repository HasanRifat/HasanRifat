## Premise

Most halal poultry is sold on trust and price, and the shop is an afterthought. Barakah starts from the other end: the quality of the bird, slaughtered by hand, and then every step a customer takes after that — what they see from the door, how they order, what they carry home.

## What I'm doing

Everything that comes before the first sale. The business itself, the name and identity, and the digital side: how the shop presents itself and how ordering should work.
