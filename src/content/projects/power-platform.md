## Premise

Public agencies run on processes that were never designed, only accumulated: a spreadsheet, a shared mailbox, a form somebody made in 2014. The work is to replace that with something people can follow without being trained on it.

## What I'm doing

Building applications, automation workflows and data tooling on the Power Platform — first for the Board of Supervisors, now at the Public Defender's office.
