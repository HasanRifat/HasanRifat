This site is a set of drawings. Each section is a sheet; the front page is the plan that locates the rest.

## The light

The shadows on the plan are computed, not drawn. A small solar-position routine works out where the sun is over Los Angeles at the moment you load the page, and each wall casts a shadow away from it. After sunset the house switches to a lamp in the courtyard. Move a pointer over the plan and you carry the light yourself.

The same routine is on the bench in the laboratory as the [Sundial](../../lab/sundial/), and the shadow engine as the [Light study](../../lab/light-study/).

## Materials

- **Type** — Fraunces for display, Hanken Grotesk for reading, IBM Plex Mono for annotations, Amiri for Arabic.
- **Build** — Astro, generating plain HTML. No client framework; the interactive parts are a few small scripts.
- **Content** — Markdown files and one JSON file. Adding a note means adding a file.
